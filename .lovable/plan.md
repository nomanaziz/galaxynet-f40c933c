
## Galaxy Net ISP Website

A modern, professional ISP website for **Galaxy Net** with the tagline **"Fast. Reliable. Affordable."** — built in both **English and Bangla**, using an **Orange + Blue** color scheme.

---

### Pages & Sections

**1. Home Page**
- **Hero Section** — Full-width banner with "Galaxy Net" branding, tagline, and a "Get Connected" CTA button. Bold orange + blue gradient background.
- **Why Choose Us** — 3–4 feature highlights (High Speed, Unlimited Data, 24/7 Support, Affordable Pricing) with icons.
- **Popular Packages Teaser** — Short preview of top packages with a "See All Plans" button.
- **About Us Snippet** — Brief company introduction with a warm image and a "Learn More" link.
- **Testimonials** — 2–3 customer quotes.
- **Footer** — Logo, links, contact info, and social icons.

---

**2. Packages / Pricing Page**
- Grid of internet packages (e.g., Basic, Standard, Premium) showing:
  - Speed (Mbps)
  - Monthly price (BDT)
  - Key features (Unlimited/FUP, Support type)
  - "Subscribe Now" button per plan
- Toggle or tabs between Home and Business plans.

---

**3. Coverage Area Page**
- A section showing the areas/zones Galaxy Net serves.
- List of covered areas displayed as styled cards or badges.
- A simple "Check Your Area" input field where users type their location.

---

**4. Contact Us Page**
- Contact form: Name, Phone, Email, Message fields + Submit button.
- Contact details: Phone number, email, office address.
- Business hours.

---

### Design Style
- **Color Scheme:** Vibrant Orange (#FF6B00) as accent + Deep Blue (#1A3C6E) as primary.
- **Typography:** Clean, modern sans-serif. Bilingual (English + Bangla) text throughout.
- **Layout:** Responsive — works on mobile and desktop.
- **Navigation:** Top navbar with logo, links, and a "Contact" CTA button. Mobile hamburger menu.
